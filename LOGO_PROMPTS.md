# AdsPro — app icon prompts (for ChatGPT / image generation)

Needed for: Meta App Review -> App settings -> App icon. 1024x1024 PNG.

## Hard constraints (these cause rejections)
- NO text or letters visible at small size (icon renders ~40px in the FB permission dialog)
- Nothing resembling Meta/Facebook branding — no infinity loop, no lowercase "f",
  avoid Facebook blue #1877F2
- Square, solid background (not transparent), 1024x1024 PNG
- Must stay readable when shrunk to 40x40

## Brand direction
Product meaning: a FEEDBACK LOOP. Lead outcomes travel back to Meta so the algorithm
learns which leads convert. Not a funnel, not a generic up-arrow.

Palette: deep navy #0F172A or indigo #1E1B4B base, single accent — emerald #10B981
(reads as "qualified / converted") or amber #F59E0B. Matches the app's existing dark-navy
primary button on light grey.

## VARIANT A — The Return Loop  (recommended)

A minimalist app icon, 1024x1024, flat vector style. A single bold circular arrow forming
a closed loop, drawn with thick rounded strokes. The arrowhead completes the circle and
angles slightly upward, suggesting improvement and return. The loop is emerald green
(#10B981) on a deep navy (#0F172A) rounded-square background. Generous padding around the
mark. Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers. Clean geometric construction, even
stroke weight, high contrast, sharp edges. Designed to remain legible at 40x40 pixels.
Modern SaaS product icon aesthetic, not a sticker, no gradients, no drop shadows, no 3D.

## VARIANT B — The Quality Filter

A minimalist app icon, 1024x1024, flat vector style. Three thick horizontal bars stacked
and progressively narrowing downward, like a simplified filter. The bottom bar is emerald
green (#10B981) while the two above are muted slate grey, showing that only the best
passes through. Rounded stroke caps, generous spacing between bars. Deep navy (#0F172A)
rounded-square background. No text, no letters, no numbers, no funnel outline. Even
weights, high contrast, geometric precision. Must stay readable at 40x40 pixels. Modern
SaaS product icon, flat, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D.

## VARIANT C — Signal Return Chevron

A minimalist app icon, 1024x1024, flat vector style. A bold upward chevron with its right
arm curving back down and around into a small arrowhead pointing back at the chevron's
base, forming a compact return-signal mark. Emerald green (#10B981) stroke on deep navy
(#0F172A) rounded-square background, thick rounded strokes, generous padding. No text, no
letters, no numbers. Geometric, symmetrical, high contrast, legible at 40x40 pixels.
Modern SaaS product icon, flat design, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D.

## How to judge the results

1. Shrink it to 40x40 (or just look at it as a browser favicon). If it turns to mush,
   reject it — that is the size Meta reviewers see it at.
2. Ask: could anyone mistake this for a Meta or Facebook product? If yes, reject.
3. Ask: does it still read on a white background AND a dark one?
4. Pick the one that is SIMPLEST. At icon size, fewer shapes always wins.

If ChatGPT adds text despite the instruction, re-run with "absolutely no typography,
no letterforms, pure geometric symbol only" appended.
