AdsPro (https://adsproindia.com) is a lead-quality feedback tool for advertisers running
Meta Lead Ads. Advertisers connect their own Meta ad account and Facebook Page. AdsPro
receives their Lead Ads submissions, lets their sales team record each lead's real outcome
(contacted, qualified, booked, purchased), and sends those outcomes back to the
advertiser's own dataset through the Conversions API, so Meta can optimise delivery toward
leads that actually convert rather than leads that merely fill in a form.

FACEBOOK LOGIN
Yes - Facebook Login for Business is integrated and actively used. It is the only way an
advertiser connects their Meta assets to AdsPro. The "Connect Meta" button on the
Dashboard starts the Facebook Login for Business flow. The returned user access token is
encrypted at rest and used to read the advertiser's ad accounts and datasets, list their
Pages, subscribe the selected Page to the leadgen webhook, and post conversion events.

STEPS TO REPRODUCE

1. Go to https://adsproindia.com/auth and sign in with the credentials above.
2. You land on the Dashboard, showing a "Connect Meta" card.
3. Type any name into the field "Workspace name (e.g. Acme Solar)". This field is required.
4. Click "Connect Meta" and authorise with a Facebook account that has an ad account and a
   Page. This is the Facebook Login for Business flow.
5. Back in AdsPro, select an ad account and a dataset from the two dropdowns, then save.
   - business_management reads the businesses and ad accounts the person already has
     access to, so advertisers whose assets sit under a Business portfolio can complete
     setup.
   - ads_management reads those ad accounts and their datasets.
6. Click "Integration" in the left sidebar.
7. In the "Facebook Page" card, open the dropdown labelled "Choose the Page your lead ads
   run from", select a Page, and confirm.
   - pages_show_list populates that dropdown with the Pages the person manages.
   - pages_read_engagement supplies the Page name displayed on the card.
   - pages_manage_metadata subscribes the Page to the leadgen webhook
     (POST /{page-id}/subscribed_apps with subscribed_fields=leadgen).
   - leads_retrieval is required for that leadgen subscription to be permitted at all.
   On success the card reads:
   "Connected - leads from this Page will arrive automatically"
   On failure AdsPro shows Meta's verbatim error and deliberately does not save the Page,
   so a broken subscription can never appear connected.
8. Still on Integration, scroll to "Send test event" and click it. Leave the optional test
   event code blank.
   - ads_management posts this server event to the selected dataset via the Conversions
     API. AdsPro never creates, edits, pauses or deletes campaigns, ad sets, ads or
     creatives, and never spends budget.
   Meta's verbatim response appears, containing "events_received": 1.
9. Click "Deliveries" in the left sidebar to see the delivery log with the Meta event name,
   HTTP status, and Meta's response body.

DATA HANDLING
AdsPro stores only identifiers - the leadgen_id, form_id, ad_id and timestamp. It does not
store lead names, email addresses or phone numbers. Meta's Conversions API accepts lead_id
as the preferred match key for lead-ads conversions, so the full optimisation loop works
without AdsPro holding any personal data. Lead records are retained for a maximum of
90 days.

REVOKING ACCESS
Settings -> Meta connection -> "Disconnect Meta" unsubscribes the Page, calls
DELETE /me/permissions to revoke every granted permission on Meta's side, and erases the
stored access token, ad account, dataset and Page. Full account and data deletion is
available at Settings -> Danger zone. Both paths are documented at
https://adsproindia.com/data-deletion
